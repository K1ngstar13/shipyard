Common fixes

  • No sound: check that your phone isn't on silent and the volume is up, then reopen the game.
  • Game looks stuck: close the game fully and open it again. Your progress is saved on your device.
  • Lost progress after reinstalling: progress is stored on the device, so uninstalling a game removes it.

# Launch checklist

Per game. Ignore the parts that don't apply.

## Before you submit

- [ ] Preflight passes with destination `review` (no red items)
- [ ] Played the TestFlight build on a real phone, wifi and cellular off
- [ ] Played it on the smallest screen you own, and one large one
- [ ] Sound stops when you switch apps, and comes back when you return
- [ ] Android back button minimizes, doesn't quit mid-game
- [ ] Progress survives closing and reopening the game
- [ ] Looked at the generated screenshots — they show gameplay, not a title screen
- [ ] Icon is legible at the size it appears on a home screen
- [ ] Description's first sentence says what the player does
- [ ] No other game's name anywhere in the text
- [ ] Privacy and support URLs load

## Submission day

- [ ] Version bumped in the panel
- [ ] What's new written in real words
- [ ] Shipped to Store review on both stores

## While waiting

- [ ] Recorded a 15–30 second screen capture of gameplay, no narration
- [ ] Written three versions of a one-line pitch
- [ ] Listed where you'll post: r/incremental_games, r/AndroidGaming, r/iosgaming, r/WebGames, Bluesky, TikTok, genre Discords

## Launch day

- [ ] Grabbed both store links
- [ ] Posted the video, not a screenshot — video gets several times the engagement
- [ ] Posted as a player sharing a thing you made, not as a company doing marketing
- [ ] Replied to every comment for the first few hours

## Week one

- [ ] Read every review on both stores
- [ ] Replied to the negative ones, politely, with what you're fixing
- [ ] Shipped one update. Both stores favor games that get updated
- [ ] Wrote down the three most requested things

## What actually moves installs for a small game

1. A good icon. It's the only thing everyone sees.
2. A short video of the game, posted where the genre's players already are.
3. The first screenshot showing the moment that makes the game fun.
4. Regular updates. Both stores weight recency.
5. Replying to reviews. It visibly lifts ratings.

Paid ads are not on this list. For a free game with no purchases, they don't pay back.
